The HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to analyze employee workforce information and identify patterns related to employee attrition.

The dashboard presents key workforce metrics and visualizations to make HR data easier to understand and support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze employee attrition patterns
Compare attrition across departments
Analyze the relationship between overtime and attrition
Study job satisfaction and employee attrition
Analyze employee income and workforce information
Create summary reports and visual dashboards

📋 Dataset

The project uses a synthetic HR dataset created for educational and portfolio purposes.

The dataset contains employee-level information including:

Employee ID
Age
Gender
Marital Status
Education
Department
Job Role
Years at Company
Total Working Years
Monthly Income
Overtime
Job Satisfaction
Environment Satisfaction
Work-Life Balance
Distance From Home
Promotions
Training Hours
Attrition

Note: The dataset does not represent real employees or real company data.

📈 Dashboard Metrics

The dashboard includes key metrics such as:

Total Employees
Total Attritions
Attrition Rate
Average Monthly Income

It also provides visual analysis of:

Attrition by Department
Attrition by Overtime
Attrition by Job Satisfaction
Department-level workforce trends
Employee and role-level summaries
🔍 Analysis Performed
Department Analysis

Compared employee counts and attrition across different departments.

Overtime Analysis

Analyzed employee attrition based on overtime status.

Job Satisfaction Analysis

Examined attrition patterns across different job satisfaction levels.

Income Analysis

Calculated and analyzed average monthly employee income.

Workforce Analysis

Used employee demographics, roles, experience, and training information to understand workforce patterns.
